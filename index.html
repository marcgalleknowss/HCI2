Application Guide

How to Apply

Follow these steps to submit your scholarship application to the Office of Scholarships, Job Placement and Alumni Relations.

Application Steps

1

Check Eligibility

Use our Eligibility Checker tool or visit OSEJPAR to confirm which scholarships you qualify for based on your GPA, units enrolled, and other criteria.

2

Gather Requirements

Prepare all required documents: grade report, enrollment form, income documents, and other scholarship-specific requirements (e.g., medical certificate for PWD).

3

Submit Application

Submit the Application for Scholarship (ASG) form to OSEJPAR, located at the Alumni Building. Students enrolled in 18+ units are automatically evaluated by the system.

4

Follow Up

Follow up with OSEJPAR after one month to verify your grantee status. Some scholarships may require an interview with the benefactor or sponsor.

5

Sign Agreement

Successful applicants will be called to sign a scholarship agreement. For alumni or corporate-sponsored grants, the benefactor may also be present for the signing.

6

Maintain Eligibility

To retain your scholarship, you must comply with the retention requirements each semester — typically maintaining GPA thresholds and having no failing grades.

Important Notes
  • Applications are accepted every semester for academic scholarships.
  • Scholars receiving another scholarship may apply for cash conversion of overlapping grants.
  • A scholar who is also a University/College grantee may only convert the difference in value.
  • NSTP, Euthenics, and bracketed subjects are not included in the GPA computation.
  • Like and follow the MSEUF OSEJPAR Facebook page for application announcements and updates.
